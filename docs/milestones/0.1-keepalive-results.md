### Reading a drop (final fix wave)

- Phone: `session-end <reason>,matured=<bool>,liveness=<bool>,s=<seconds connected>`. Reasons: `EOF` (the Mac closed), `GOODBYE_<reason>`, `PING_TIMEOUT` (no answer to the phone's ping), `IO_<Class>` / `TLS_<Class>` (transport error), `ERROR_<code>`, `LOCAL_CLOSE`, `CANCELLED` (the service stopped).
- Mac: `phone session ended: <code>`, one of `silence` (75 s rule, no `liveness.v1`), `liveness` (3 silent ping intervals), `eof`, `error`, `goodbye`, `wake` (no `liveness.v1`), `replaced`, `stop`, `unpaired`, `rejected`, `pairing`.
- A Keystore failure while the phone signs its TLS certificate now counts as a transient failure (backoff), not as the Mac refusing the phone. It is recognised by a security exception in the cause chain or BoringSSL's `PRIVATE_KEY_OPERATION_FAILED`; another form would still count as a refusal (two in a row give Not recognized, left by a kick or after 15 min).
