What changes

  • A transfer that breaks (Wi-Fi drop, Mac sleep, the phone out of range) shows Paused on both devices, keeping its progress.
  • When they reconnect it resumes on its own from the last byte saved. Nothing to tap.
  • The half-received file waits 24 hours. After that it is deleted and the next try starts over.
  • Cancel on either side ends it for good and deletes the half-received file.
  • The whole file is still checked (SHA-256) at the end, so a resumed file is the same as one sent in one go.
  • Works for files you send either way (drag, Share menu, Send to Mac). Photos, screenshots and Finder browsing keep restarting: they are small or re-asked anyway.
