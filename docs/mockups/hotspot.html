How it works

  • While the two are connected (for example on Wi-Fi with no internet), the Mac menu's Use Phone Hotspot turns the hotspot on and joins it. The two stay connected over it.
  • With no Wi-Fi at all, the Mac can't reach the phone (and Full mode needs Wi-Fi), so you turn the hotspot on with Android's own Hotspot tile. The Mac knows the hotspot from an earlier connect: Join Pixel's Hotspot in its menu.
  • Turning the hotspot on from the Mac needs Full mode (the same one-time setup as mirroring).
  • Turn Off Hotspot on either side turns it off; the Mac goes back to its usual Wi-Fi.
  • The Mac asks once for Location access: macOS hides Wi-Fi names from apps without it.
