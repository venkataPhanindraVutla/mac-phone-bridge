What this shows

  • Every phone notification that reached the Mac is kept, newest first, grouped by day, even after you swipe it away on either device.
  • A green dot means it is still on the phone. Updates to the same notification (a chat that grows) replace its row instead of adding new ones.
  • Click a row to read all of it. Copy Text and, when there is a one-time code, Copy Code.
  • Search looks in the app name, title and text. The menu lets you show one app.
  • Settings: keep it off, 1, 7 or 30 days (7 by default), and Clear History…. Pictures are not kept, only text.
