Notes — on your Mac
  • Same keys as your MacBook Air (US layout, ABC – India input). The phone sends the Mac's own key codes, so shortcuts and ₹ (⌥4) work exactly like the built-in keyboard.
  • ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ fn: tap once for the next key only, tap twice to lock, or hold with one thumb and tap with the other.
  • Collapse: hide either half to use the other full-screen; tap the chip to bring it back.
  • Sounds: a short synthesized key click (our own, not a copied file). Haptics: a 10 ms tick per key, Android only.
