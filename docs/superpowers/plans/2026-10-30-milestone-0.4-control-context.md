# Milestone 0.4 (Control & Context) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Whatever plays on the phone shows in the Mac menu, the Phone window and macOS Now Playing, and the Mac controls it (play/pause, next, previous, seek, volume), with commands taking effect in under 300 ms. Do Not Disturb on the phone and Focus on the Mac follow each other within 5 s once the two Shortcuts are installed. The Mac locks itself when the paired phone's Bluetooth signal stays away, with no false locks in 8 h of desk use. The phone works as a trackpad and a presentation remote with a laser dot.

**Architecture:**
- **Protocol.** Four new channels and capabilities (`media.v1`, `dnd.v1`, `proximity.v1`, `input.v1`) in `.proto` and SPEC.md §20–§23, like 0.3's features. Everything rides the control connection; no bulk connections. The only byte formats outside protobuf are the BLE ID and the artwork key, and both get vectors.
- **Phone.** Decision logic lives in JVM-testable classes in four new Android library modules: `feature/media`, `feature/dnd`, `feature/proximity` and `feature/input`. The Android adapters (MediaSessionManager, NotificationManager, BluetoothLeAdvertiser, the Compose gesture surface) sit beside them behind small interfaces. `ConnectionService` and `AppGraph` wire them with 0.3's gating pattern. The trackpad is a new `TrackpadActivity`.
- **Mac.** Four new AMKit targets: `FeatureMedia`, `FeatureFocus`, `FeatureProximity` and `FeatureInput`. Each model conforms to `SessionFeature` and is registered with `NotifyWorkQueue`. System APIs (MediaPlayer, Intents `INFocusStatusCenter`, `/usr/bin/shortcuts`, CoreBluetooth, `CGEvent`, the lock symbol) sit behind protocols, so the models are tested with fakes. Proximity also runs while no phone is connected: it needs only the paired phones and the discovery key.
- **No connection-core logic changes.** `ConnectionManager`, `PhoneSession`, `MacServer` and `ControlConnection` stay as reviewed.

**Tech Stack:** as 0.3. New APIs:
- **Android:** `MediaSessionManager`, `MediaController` (+ `Callback`, `TransportControls`, `PlaybackInfo`), `AudioManager`, `NotificationManager` interruption filters and `ACTION_INTERRUPTION_FILTER_CHANGED`, `BluetoothLeAdvertiser`, Compose `pointerInput`.
- **Mac:** `MediaPlayer` (`MPNowPlayingInfoCenter`, `MPRemoteCommandCenter`), `Intents` (`INFocusStatusCenter`), `Process` (`/usr/bin/shortcuts`), `CoreBluetooth` (`CBCentralManager`), `CoreGraphics` (`CGEvent`, `CGEventSource`, `CGSessionCopyCurrentDictionary`), `ApplicationServices` (`AXIsProcessTrustedWithOptions`), `dlopen`/`dlsym`.

No new third-party dependencies.

**Spec:** [docs/PRD.md](../../PRD.md) (F-07, F-11, F-15, F-16, §8, §9, §11), [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) (§3.1, §4, §5, §7, §9), [docs/ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) §0.4. Protocol rules: [protocol/SPEC.md](../../../protocol/SPEC.md). The controller's design decisions are binding, and this plan follows them except where the notes below say otherwise. This plan builds on branch `milestone-0.4` at `6d8a372` (0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 complete).

**Where this plan departs from the design notes, and why:**
1. **The BLE ID also hashes the phone's fingerprint:** `HMAC-SHA256(discovery_key, "anmirror-ble-v1" || phone_fp || uint64_be(epoch))[0..8]`. SPEC §5.1 says the discovery key is "generated once per Mac", so it is the same for every phone paired with that Mac. Without `phone_fp`, two paired phones would advertise the same ID, and the Mac could not tell which one is near.
2. **The Mac scans with `withServices: nil` and filters on the service data.** A legacy advertisement holds 31 bytes, and the 128-bit service-data element alone takes 26 of them. That leaves no room for a service-UUID list, so the advertisement carries only the service data, and CoreBluetooth's service filter cannot match it.
3. **The seek and volume sliders live in a new "Now Playing" tab in the Phone window, not in the menu.** The menu-style `MenuBarExtra` (0.1–0.3) renders only buttons, toggles, pickers, text and submenus, so a slider in it does not appear. Moving the whole menu to window style would rework every earlier menu item. The menu gets transport buttons and "Volume Up" / "Volume Down"; Control Center's Now Playing also has a seek bar.
4. **`dnd.availability` goes both ways, as `DndAvailability{sends, applies}`, and `DndState` gains `snapshot`.**
   - Ruling R22 makes the Mac's automatic DND stream gate on the phone's availability. The phone can report its changes without any permission, but it can apply the Mac's only with policy access, so one `enabled` flag cannot carry both facts.
   - Both UIs must say which directions are active.
   - `snapshot` marks the state sent at connect, which the design notes say is shown but not applied.
5. **Two new `AvailabilityReason` values:** `UNAVAILABLE = 4` (Bluetooth is off, BLE advertising is unsupported, or Focus status can't be read in this build) and `NEEDS_SETUP = 5` (the Mac's Shortcuts are missing). Without them, these states would be reported under a wrong reason.
6. **The 5 s stuck-button release counts from the last `input.*` frame while a button is held,** not from the button-down. Otherwise a slow drag longer than 5 s, such as moving a window across two screens, would be dropped mid-way. The goal, "no stuck buttons", still holds.
7. **Auto-lock arms only after the phone has been seen NEAR,** and it uses the phone's last `proximity.availability`, kept per phone in `UserDefaults`, while disconnected. This extends the design's re-arm rule to app launch and to turning the feature on, so a phone that is already away, or whose switch is off, never causes a lock.
8. **The phone advertises for one Mac:** the connected one, or else the paired Mac with the latest `lastConnectedAt`. A phone connects to one Mac at a time (PRD §11), and one advertisement keeps battery use low.
9. **Test mode ignores the "no local input" condition.** The person testing is at the keyboard, so the countdown would never reach "Would lock now" otherwise. The idle time is shown beside it.
10. Copy: the design's "Check again" button is written "Check Again", in macOS title case. Build rulings continue the numbering from 0.3, starting at **R25**.

## Global Constraints

Everything in the 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 plans' Global Constraints still applies (platforms, licensing, TLS, identifiers, strings, accessibility, gating). In addition:

**Hard rules carried from 0.1–0.3** (from the design notes, word for word where they give exact rules)
- **Do NOT change ConnectionManager / PhoneSession / MacServer / ControlConnection logic.** New channels and message types are OK.
- **SPEC §3: no feature message unless the peer lists the capability.**
  - **Phone gating:** shared + user switch on + permission granted, via `featureAvailability(granted, userOn)`; `<feature>.availability` is always allowed.
  - **Mac (ruling R22 from 0.3):** every AUTOMATIC Mac→phone stream also gates on the phone's reported availability (nil = off).
- **Mac:** phone messages go through the single ordered NotifyWorkQueue (`register`, `SessionFeature`). Never block it on UI, authorization, IPC or subprocesses (`shortcuts run` must run off the queue).
- **Never read SMS.**
- **No content in logs.** Track titles, artists and app names count as content. Treat app labels and package names as content too, and log only IDs, counts and codes. RSSI values, durations and enum names are fine.
- **Permissions are asked for only when the feature's switch is turned on.**
  - **Android:** each permission is requested only when its feature is turned on.
  - **Mac:** each TCC permission (Bluetooth, Accessibility, Focus status) is requested only when its Mac setting is turned on.
- **Copy written by implementers is listed for owner review in the results doc** (as in 0.3).
- **Never unlocks the Mac** (PRD F-15).

**Capabilities**
- The Mac and the phone each add `media.v1`, `dnd.v1`, `proximity.v1` and `input.v1` to their capability lists, each in the task that wires the feature. The sims add them only behind their flags.
- **Which availability message each side sends:**
  - **Phone to Mac:** `media.availability`, `dnd.availability` and `proximity.availability`.
  - **Mac to phone:** `dnd.availability` and `input.availability`.
  - Each is sent on connect (when the capability is shared) and on change.

**Wire limits and timing**
- **A frame is at most 1,048,576 bytes** (SPEC §2).
- **Media:**
  - `media.state` is sent on change, at most 4 per second, and right after connect.
    - **Throttle:** the first change after 250 ms of quiet goes at once. Later changes inside the 250 ms coalesce, and the latest state wins (leading and trailing edges).
    - **Text:** `title`, `artist`, `album` and `app_label` are each at most 256 bytes, cut at a UTF-8 boundary with "…" (SPEC §10's rule; use `NotificationMapper.truncateUtf8`).
  - **Artwork:**
    - JPEG, long edge 300 px.
    - At most 96 KB (98,304 bytes): encode at quality 85, then 70, 55 and 40 until it fits, and drop it if it still doesn't.
    - Sent once per new key.
  - **Artwork key:** lowercase hex of `SHA-256(jpeg)[0..8]` (16 characters), or empty when there is no artwork.
  - **Mac artwork cache:** in memory only, the 8 most recent keys, cleared on disconnect.
- **DND:**
  - The Mac polls Focus every 2 s while connected and the feature is on.
  - The echo window is 10 s.
  - **Shortcut names, exactly:** "Android Mirroring Focus On" and "Android Mirroring Focus Off".
  - `/usr/bin/shortcuts run` has a 10 s timeout.
  - `/usr/bin/shortcuts list` runs on feature enable, on connect and on "Check Again".
- **Proximity:**
  - **Service UUID:** `3d30288c-0f47-4016-a6ba-44506f920431`.
  - **The advertisement:** service data under that UUID = the 8-byte BLE ID, and nothing else. `ADVERTISE_MODE_LOW_POWER`, `ADVERTISE_TX_POWER_MEDIUM`, connectable false, no device name, no TX power level.
  - **Epochs:** `epoch = floor(unix/900)`. The phone restarts the advertisement at each boundary, and the Mac accepts `epoch-1..epoch+1`.
  - **Smoothing:** the median of the last 10 s of samples (for an even count, the higher of the two middle values). "Absent" after 20 s with no sample.
  - **FAR** = smoothed RSSI below the threshold, or absent.
  - **Threshold:** default −80 dBm, adjustable −95…−55 in steps of 1.
  - **Delay:** default 30 s, adjustable 10–120 s in steps of 5.
  - **Evaluation:** every 1 s while the feature is on.
- **Input:**
  - The phone coalesces moves and scrolls to at most 120 Hz: deltas are summed, never queued.
  - The Mac drops `input.*` frames beyond 240 in any 1 s window, but never drops `input.button{down: false}` or `input.laser{on: false}`.
  - A held button is released 5 s after the last `input.*` frame, and on disconnect.
  - Accessibility is re-checked on app activation, and every 2 s while the setting is on and a phone is connected.
  - **Laser dot:** 24 pt circle, `NSColor.systemRed` at 60 % alpha.

**Acceptance targets** (PRD, ROADMAP 0.4 exit)
- **Media:**
  - Commands take effect on the phone in under 300 ms. Interop case 9 measures the loopback round trip; the device figure is an owner check.
  - The Mac log `media` category records `command <kind> rtt <ms> ms` for each command, measured to the first `media.state` after it from that session.
- **DND:** both directions within 5 s, once the Shortcuts are installed.
- **Auto-lock:** no false locks in 8 h of normal desk use at the default setting.
  - The Mac never locks while someone is using it: no lock unless `CGEventSource.secondsSinceLastEventType(.combinedSessionState, kCGAnyInputEventType)` ≥ the delay.
  - The `proximity` log category records every lock with the smoothed RSSI, for the owner's 8 h count.

## Review Focus

These are the five failures a real person is most likely to hit that no feature test covers. Each one gets a test in the task named in brackets.

1. **A false auto-lock at the desk.** The phone is in a pocket or bag, the person is reading without typing, the Mac just woke, the epoch has just rolled over, a second paired phone is nearby, or the phone's switch or Bluetooth is off. Expected:
   - No lock while there was local input within the delay.
   - A single weak sample doesn't lock (median).
   - No lock before the phone has been seen near since launch or since the feature was enabled.
   - After a lock, nothing more until the phone is near again.
   - The previous epoch's ID still matches.
   - Another paired phone's beacon doesn't count.
   - Nothing happens while the screen is locked or asleep, Bluetooth is off or unauthorized, or the phone's last availability is off.

   *(Task 7 `neverLocksWhileMacIsInUse`, `singleWeakSampleDoesNotLock`, `noLockBeforeFirstNear`, `lockDisarmsUntilNearAgain`, `previousEpochIdStillMatches`, `otherPairedPhoneDoesNotCountAsNear`, `blockersNeverLock`, `phoneSwitchOffNeverLocks`, `wakeClearsSamples`; Task 6 `restartsWithNewIdAtEpochBoundary`; Task 1 `bleIdVectors`)*
2. **DND and Focus bouncing back and forth.** Expected:
   - A state one side applied from the other is not sent back within 10 s.
   - A late echo goes out as `SYNC` and is not applied.
   - The connect snapshot is never applied.
   - Find my phone swapping NONE to ALARMS is not a transition.
   - A hung or missing Shortcut never blocks the Mac's queue and times out after 10 s.

   *(Task 4 `appliedMacStateIsNotEchoed`, `lateEchoIsSentAsSyncAndNotApplied`, `snapshotIsNotApplied`, `ringSwapNoneToAlarmsIsNoTransition`; Task 5 `appliedPhoneStateIsNotEchoed`, `syncOriginIsNotApplied`, `snapshotIsNotApplied`, `applyingDoesNotWaitForShortcut`, `hangingShortcutTimesOutAfter10s`)*
3. **A stuck mouse button.** The phone app is killed mid-drag, Wi-Fi drops, a flood of moves fills the rate limit just as the finger lifts, or the activity goes to the background mid-drag. Expected:
   - The Mac releases a held button 5 s after the last frame and at once on disconnect.
   - A button-up and a laser-off are never rate-limited.
   - The phone sends the up and laser-off when the finger lifts or the activity stops.

   *(Task 9 `heldButtonReleasesAfter5sOfSilence`, `disconnectReleasesHeldButtonAndHidesLaser`, `buttonUpIsNeverRateLimited`, `longDragWithMovesIsNotReleased`; Task 8 `liftAfterDragAlwaysSendsUp`, `closeReleasesHeldButtonAndLaser`)*
4. **Odd media sessions.** Two apps have sessions, the playing app is killed, the phone is casting with remote volume, a live radio stream has no seek, a command arrives for a session that just changed, the artwork is huge, or the position ticks fast. Expected:
   - The playing session (most recent) wins.
   - A killed session gives `media.state{active: false}`, and the Mac clears Now Playing.
   - Remote volume uses `playbackInfo`.
   - A stream without seek shows no seek.
   - A stale command is ignored.
   - The artwork fits in 96 KB or is dropped.
   - At most 4 states per second.

   *(Task 2 `playingSessionWinsOverRecent`, `mostRecentPlayingWins`, `sessionGoneSendsInactive`, `remoteSessionUsesPlaybackInfoVolume`, `liveStreamHasNoSeek`, `staleSessionCommandIsIgnored`, `artworkIsReencodedOrDropped`, `atMostFourStatesPerSecond`; Task 3 `inactiveClearsNowPlaying`, `seekDisabledWithoutSeekAction`)*
5. **Several monitors and flicks.** The pointer is flicked past the edge of a smaller second screen, or 1,000 moves arrive in a burst. Expected:
   - The pointer stops at the nearest point of a real screen, never in the gap between screens.
   - Every move is applied in order through the queue.
   - The phone sums the deltas instead of queueing a backlog.

   *(Task 9 `pointerClampsToNearestScreen`, `thousandMovesApplyInOrder`, `framesBeyond240PerSecondAreDropped`; Task 8 `movesAreSummedNotQueued`)*

## Order and parallelism

- **Task 1** (protocol, both platforms) comes first. Everything else depends on it.
- **Android lane:** 2 → 4 → 6 → 8. Each task modifies `AppGraph.kt`, `ConnectionService.kt`, `HomeScreen.kt`, `strings.xml`, `settings.gradle.kts` and `app/build.gradle.kts`, so they run in order.

- **Can run in parallel** (different platforms, disjoint files, separate worktrees): **2 ∥ 3**, **4 ∥ 5**, **6 ∥ 7**, **8 ∥ 9**. The two lanes may also run fully independently. The one cross-lane link is Task 3's last step (interop case 9), which needs Task 2's `phone-sim --media` merged first.
- **Task 10** comes last.

- Produces:

```swift
@MainActor public protocol EventPoster: AnyObject {
    func location() -> CGPoint                                   // global CG coordinates, top-left origin
    func screens() -> [CGRect]                                   // the same space
    func move(to p: CGPoint, holding: AMMouseButton?)            // .mouseMoved, or .leftMouseDragged / .rightMouseDragged
    func button(_ b: AMMouseButton, down: Bool, at p: CGPoint, clickCount: Int)
    func scroll(pixelsX: Int32, pixelsY: Int32)                  // scrollWheelEvent2, units .pixel
    func key(_ code: CGKeyCode)                                  // down then up; 123 Left, 124 Right
}
@MainActor public protocol LaserOverlay: AnyObject { func show(at p: CGPoint); func hide() }
@MainActor public protocol AccessibilityTrust: AnyObject { func isTrusted(prompt: Bool) -> Bool }
public enum PointerMath { public static func clamp(_ p: CGPoint, to screens: [CGRect]) -> CGPoint }   // inside any → p; else nearest point of nearest rect
public struct RateWindow {
    public init(limit: Int = 240, span: TimeInterval = 1)
    public mutating func admit(at: Date) -> Bool
}
@MainActor @Observable public final class InputModel: SessionFeature {     // ["input."]
    public init(poster: any EventPoster, laser: any LaserOverlay, trust: any AccessibilityTrust, enabled: Bool,
                naturalScrolling: @escaping () -> Bool,
                send: @escaping @MainActor (MessageType, any SwiftProtobuf.Message & Sendable) async -> Void,
                now: @escaping () -> Date = Date.init, releaseAfter: TimeInterval = 5)
    public private(set) var enabled: Bool; public private(set) var trusted: Bool
    public func setEnabled(_ on: Bool) async                     // on: isTrusted(prompt: true) (only now); sends availability
    public func recheckTrust() async                             // isTrusted(prompt: false); sends availability on change
    public func tick()                                           // auto-release; a 1 s timer while a button is held
}
```

**Rules:**
- **`input.availability`:** `OFF_BY_USER` when disabled, `NEEDS_PERMISSION` when not trusted, else enabled. It is sent on connect (when `input.v1` is shared) and on change.
- **Which frames act:**
  - `input.*` acts only while enabled and trusted, and each frame must pass `RateWindow.admit`.
  - `input.button{down: false}` and `input.laser{on: false}` skip the rate window, but still count toward it.
- **Each frame:**
  - **Move:** `PointerMath.clamp(location + (dx, dy), screens)`, then `move(to:holding:)` with any held button. While the laser is on, the overlay follows.
  - **Button:** `button(...)` at the current location; the model tracks the held button.
  - **Click:** a down and an up with `clickCount = count`.
  - **Scroll:** `scroll` with `pixelsY = round(dy)` and `pixelsX = round(dx)`, both negated when `naturalScrolling()` is false. `naturalScrolling` reads `com.apple.swipescrolldirection` from the global domain, default true.
  - **Key:** `PREV_SLIDE` → 123, `NEXT_SLIDE` → 124.
  - **Laser:** on → `laser.show(at: location)`; off → `hide()`.
- **Releases:**
  - Every frame stamps `lastFrameAt`. `tick()` releases a held button (an up at the current location) and hides the laser when `now − lastFrameAt ≥ 5 s`.
  - `disconnected()` does the same at once.
- **Accessibility:**
  - `recheckTrust` runs on app activation and from a 2 s loop while enabled and connected.
  - Losing trust releases any held button through the poster. The post may fail, which is harmless. The model then sends `NEEDS_PERMISSION`.
- **`CGEventPoster`:**
  - `CGEvent(mouseEventSource:mouseType:mouseCursorPosition:mouseButton:)` with `.mouseEventClickState` set to the click count, posted to `.cghidEventTap`.
  - **Screens:** `NSScreen.screens` frames flipped to CG global coordinates, against the primary screen's height.
- **`LaserOverlayPanel`:**
  - A borderless, transparent `NSPanel`, `ignoresMouseEvents = true`, level `.screenSaver`, `collectionBehavior` `[.canJoinAllSpaces, .stationary, .ignoresCycle]`.
  - It holds a 24 pt circle filled `systemRed` at alpha 0.6, centred on the pointer.
- **Routing:** `input.*` goes through `NotifyWorkQueue` like every phone message, which keeps down, move and up in order. Handlers do no I/O beyond the poster.
